# SFT training-data analysis (teacher distillation on four datasets)

_Generated by `utils/sft_training_analysis.py` on 2026-10-20. Total evaluated records: 51319._

All four logs are Qwen3.8-27B teacher runs on the **training** splits, i.e. the pool from which SFT traces are distilled. A trace is **positive** under the repository's definition when the strict evaluator marks it correct and it retrieved at least one triple (`utils/filter_positive_training_samples.py`). Two broader variants are reported as well: **verified** positives (strict or semantically equivalent under `utils/answer_normalization.verify_answer`) and **usable** positives (verified, tool-using, complete and not truncated).

## 8. Takeaways for the SFT mixture

- The four runs yield **17176 strict positives** and **20973 verified positives**; every verified positive is usable as-is (non-truncated and complete).
- Verification recovers **3798** answers (formatting/alias/date artifacts), so the strict evaluator under-counts correct traces.
- **24896** traces (48.5%) hit the token limit and cannot be used as supervision at the current budget.
- Only **257** positives use the count tool and **3348** use the sentinel: the behaviours the paper wants to teach are barely covered by this natural pool.
